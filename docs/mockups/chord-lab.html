MOCK-UP · FIVE STACKED TOOLS
  1. STEP 0101

    Look it up

    Pick a chord and see every way to play it, on a box and on the neck.

  2. STEP 0202

    Name it

    Tap a shape you know onto the neck; it says what chord it is and which keys it lives in.

  3. STEP 0303

    Build a loop

    Tap chords from your key into a loop; it suggests what comes next.

  4. STEP 0404

    Move it

    Find the key, then shift it: another key, a capo or a drop tuning.
