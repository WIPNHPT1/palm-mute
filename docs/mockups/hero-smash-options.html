Smash it: six ways to put a guitarist behind the hero

The hero stays exactly as it is: the headline, the glass card, the studio light. The guitarist stands in the gap between the headline and the card, behind both; in light mode the silhouette is a soft grey so the headline stays readable over it. On a canvas under everything, a guitarist winds up and smashes a Les Paul into the stage on the downbeat, every two bars at 180 BPM: the body splits, splinters and snapped strings fly, dust kicks up. The figure is drawn at real proportions (a 180 cm player, a 100 cm guitar) and shot like a live photo: backlight, rim light, haze, motion blur, grain. Six photographic treatments. It's decoration only (hidden from screen readers), freezes on the windup with reduced motion, and only draws while on screen.
