1 · Zoom

Make it read closer on desktop

The page is built at a fixed size and stops growing at 1440px, so on a 1920 or 2560 monitor everything looks small and far away. Each option scales the whole page (text, controls, tabs and diagrams together, so nothing breaks) above a given width. Phones and tablets stay exactly as they are. More zoom means narrower side gutters, so zoom and gutters are chosen together.

2 · Side gutters

What fills the paper either side on desktop

Shown only where there's real room (more than about 100px each side), never on tablets or phones, always behind the page and never clickable, so they can't get in the way. The ones marked reactive move with the music when something plays.

3 · Moving background

Behind the Song Generator and the Chord Lab

Drawn on one canvas behind the page, very faint, at 30 frames a second, paused when the tab is hidden and frozen to a still frame for anyone who has reduced motion switched on. Cards stay solid so tabs and text never sit on moving pixels. You could use one for both pages or give each its own.

4 · Menu button

Hover it, then press it

The current button is two lines that spin into a cross. Each option keeps a 44px target, an accessible name and the open/closed state, and all of them turn into a clear close mark.

5 · Extras

Pick as many as you like

Small moments that make the site feel made for musicians. Each is optional and independent. All of them switch off for reduced motion and on touch screens where they don't make sense.
