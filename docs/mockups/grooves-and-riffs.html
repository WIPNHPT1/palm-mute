1 · How should a 16th-note bar look in a card?

A gallop needs 16 cells a bar; today every bar has 8. Switch the layout at the top; every tab on the page follows. A: every bar 16 cells, 2 per line (the text shrinks). B: 16 cells, 1 bar per line (text stays big, tabs get twice as tall). C: a bar only gets 16 cells when it needs them; eighth-note bars stay 8. D (recommended): like C, but a 16th-note bar gets a line to itself, so it stays readable on a phone; today's tabs don't change at all. The brass number is the tab's text size in a 288px phone card (the site's floor for tab art is about 7px).
