DESIGN OPTIONS

Making it feel expensive

Fourteen components from the Generator and the Chord Lab, each shown as it ships today and at three levels of finish. Hover, press, tab and play with every one. Pick the level you like for each and your list collects at the bottom.

Colours: untouched

Every colour is an existing token. Highlights and shadows are translucent tints of those same tokens, in both themes.

Layout: untouched

Same sizes, same grid, same 44px targets, same 12px text. Depth and detail come from shadows and pseudo-elements, which never move anything.

Type: untouched

Archivo Black, Space Grotesk and Space Mono stay. Only tracking, numerals and a few small details change.

Cost: CSS

Nearly everything is plain CSS. The segmented slider is the one place that needs a few lines of script.
